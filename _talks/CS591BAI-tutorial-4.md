In this section, we shift our focus from structural interpretability to behavioral interpretability by introducing Test-Time Compute (System 2 Thinking), Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning, and Competitive Market-Based Learning, and illustrating how structural and behavioral interpretability form a coherent and complementary framework.
